sections:
- id: challenge
  title: Challenge
  body: Tokenizing a regulated fund means the onchain representation has to mirror the legal structure exactly, support controlled transfers, and remain auditable for regulators, all while being efficient enough to run at scale.
- id: solution
  title: Solution
  body: |-
    We designed a compliance-aware token architecture that ties onchain units to the fund structure, supports transfer restrictions, and gives the administrator the controls they need without weakening the security model.

    The engagement ran as a fixed-scope, milestone-based integration: one scoping session with the bank's technical lead, an MOU with verifiable milestones, a first release on testnet with the operations team, then mainnet deployment, runbooks and handover.
- id: architecture
  title: Architecture highlights
  body: |-
    - Onchain units mapped one-to-one to the fund's legal structure
    - Transfer restrictions enforced at the contract layer
    - Administrator controls separated from holder operations
    - Auditable issuance and redemption flows
- id: outcome
  title: Outcome
  body: €130M of fund assets tokenized and live on Stellar mainnet, with subscriptions and redemptions running onchain under the bank's existing compliance controls. The administrator operates the fund without an engineer in the loop.
