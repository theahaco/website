Chad moved into Web3 in 2019 after years in web startups and consulting, building Ethereum DAO applications and then working on NEAR Protocol. He co-founded The Aha Company with [Willem](../willem-wyndham) in 2021.

Find Chad presenting at various conferences and broadcasts, simplifying complex ideas and making them exciting for a general audience: [Stellar Registry](../../open-source/stellar-registry) at [a Stellar Community Call](https://x.com/BuildOnStellar/status/2119930043858333899), Aha's [Moonlight](../../open-source/moonlight) privacy layer [at Meridian '26](https://youtu.be/_PuD5qT_xUE?si=dLHtErJ-xT-fFrj9&t=405), [Stellar Scaffold](../../open-source/stellar-scaffold) [at Meridian '25](https://meridian.stellar.org/sessions/rapid-application-development-with-scaffold-stellar), and [joy-centered developer tooling at Meridian '24](https://meridian.stellar.org/sessions/how-stellars-dapp-tooling-optimizes-for-joy).
