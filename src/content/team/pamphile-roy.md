Pamphile created Tansu, an onchain record of which code was released and who approved it, with governance for each project, on Stellar mainnet since 2025. He built GhostSig, a passkey wallet for XRPL and Stellar that ships as a default module in the Stellar Wallets Kit and in XRPL Commons' xrpl-connect, and co-authored the Stellar standards SEP-53 (final) and SEP-52.

Outside blockchain he is a SciPy core maintainer and sits on the Scientific Python steering committee.
